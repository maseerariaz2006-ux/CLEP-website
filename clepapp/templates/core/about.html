About CLEP

CLEP is a university-based NGO started in 2013 by DVM seniors of the University of Agriculture Faisalabad. We adopt children who are at risk of child labor and support them through education and care.

How it works

Every student contributes just Rs 5 per day. Together, these small amounts fund the education, health and growth of the children we have adopted since 2013.
